FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers

Everything you need to know about Indie Playbook, data privacy, and usage.

Which platforms are supported?

Indie Playbook is built for Android and iOS devices, with desktop and web companions supported out of the box.

How does Indie Playbook protect user privacy?

We prioritize privacy: all personal data is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS, minimal telemetry is collected for crash diagnostics, and users can request account and data deletion at any time directly in the app.

How do I request account and data deletion?

You can delete your account and associated data directly within the app under Profile/Settings > Delete Account, or by contacting our support team at .

Can I use the app for free?

Yes! Core features are accessible for free. Optional premium upgrades and subscriptions unlock advanced tools and unlimited access.
