FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers

Everything you need to know about Indie Playbook, your data, and how to get help.

Is Indie Playbook free?

Yes. Indie Playbook is free to download and use, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Where does the data come from?

Rankings, ratings, and download estimates are based on App Store and Google Play ranking data from AppFigures. Origin stories and growth playbooks are researched and curated, and the community can suggest edits.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse as a guest. Signing in with an email address is optional and lets you submit apps and suggest edits.

How does Indie Playbook protect my privacy?

We collect only what's needed to run the app: your email if you sign in, and crash diagnostics. We don't sell or share your data with third parties, and everything is sent over HTTPS. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account and data?

Delete your account directly in the app from your Profile / Settings, or email admin@dyor.com and we'll take care of it.
