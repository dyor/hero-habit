# UI/UX Strategy & Visual Design — Habit Hero

## 1. Visual Theme & Aesthetics
- **Theme**: Bold Comic Book Pop-Art with modern sleek Compose material styling.
- **Palette**:
  - Primary: Hero Blue (`#1E88E5`)
  - Accent: Comic Gold / Yellow (`#FFD54F`)
  - Energy / Action: Comic Crimson (`#E53935`)
  - Background: Dark Mode (`#121212`), Surface (`#1E1E1E`)

## 2. Typography & Micro-Interactions
- Headlines: Bold punchy typography for streak numbers and superhero titles.
- Comic speech bubble motifs for completion toasts ("Way to go!").
- Dynamic confetti & explosion burst animation on habit completion.
